ℹ️ This Privacy Policy explains how Axsls Ticket System (“the Bot”) collects, stores, and uses data when operating in your Discord server.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord User IDs, usernames, and discriminators of participants
  • Discord Server IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs
  • Ticket contents (messages, attachments, status updates)
  • Configuration data (e.g. logging channel, ticket categories, permissions)
  • Timestamps and metadata (ticket creation/closure times, moderator actions)

2. How We Use the Information

  • To provide ticketing and support features
  • To maintain logs for moderation and accountability
  • To improve reliability, uptime, and performance of the Bot
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent abuse or malicious activity

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data with any third parties.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • Data is stored securely on managed servers with restricted access
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary for the intended purpose
  • When the Bot is removed from a server, configuration and ticket logs may be stored temporarily for abuse prevention unless deletion is requested
  • Backups may persist for a limited period before permanent deletion

4. Data Deletion Requests

Server owners may request data deletion by contacting us via the official support Discord server. Requests must be verified to ensure they come from the legitimate server owner. Upon verification, we will remove stored data related to the requesting server within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Sharing of Data

  • We do not share personal data with third parties
  • We may disclose limited data if required by law or to comply with legal processes
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable statistics (e.g. uptime, usage counts) may be published publicly

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard collected data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from such users without proper consent, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time
  • Updates will be announced in the official support Discord server
  • Continued use of the Bot after updates means you accept the revised Privacy Policy

9. Contact

For privacy concerns, data requests, or legal inquiries, please contact us via the official support Discord server:
